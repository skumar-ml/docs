## Non-technical

### Overview

The [Supplementary Programs](https://www.nsdebatecamp.com/portal/supplementary-programs) page page allows users to view all enrolled additional programs such as tutoring hours or care packages. Users can also add new programs by selecting from available options and registering directly from this page.

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-189.png)
</Frame>

Clicking **Register** navigates to the program’s registration page, while **Learn More** opens the detailed information page for that program.
