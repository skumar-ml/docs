## Non-technical

### Overview

The [Family Members](https://www.nsdebatecamp.com/portal/add-family-member) page allows users to view, add, edit, and delete linked family member accounts. 

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-188.png)
</Frame>

Clicking **Add Family Member** opens a popup to enter basic details, while the **Edit** option allows updating member information such as name, email, and phone number. A **Delete** feature is also available to remove a family member account.
