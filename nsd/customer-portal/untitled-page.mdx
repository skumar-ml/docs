## Non-technical

### Overview

The [Family Members](https://www.nsdebatecamp.com/portal/add-family-member) page allows users to view, add, edit, and delete linked family member accounts.

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-188.png)
</Frame>

- Clicking **Add Family Member** opens a popup to enter basic details.

  <Frame>
    ![Image](/images/image-191.png)
  </Frame>
- The **Edit** option allows updating member information such as name, email, and phone number.

  <Frame>
    ![Image](/images/image-192.png)
  </Frame>
- A **Delete** feature is available to remove a family member account.
