## Parent Portal – Free vs. Registered User View

### 1. Overview

The Parent Portal provides a central place for parents to manage their student’s programs, forms, and resources.\
It shows all registered students along with their enrolled programs such as camps and tutoring (for paid/registered users).

### 2. Free User View (No Camp Registration)

If the parent has not purchased or registered for any camp:

- Displays **Free Resources** (articles, debate guides, educational content). This is a read-only, informational view.

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-185.png)
</Frame>

### 3. Registered / Paid User View (Camp Purchased)

If the parent has completed camp registration and payment:

- Displays enrolled student(s) and purchased programs.
- Enables Registration Forms, Check-In, Tutoring access, and Invoice/Payment tracking.

This is a full program management dashboard.

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-181.png)
</Frame>

### Camp Tab

Each student card displays enrolled programs (e.g., Flagship LD, Texas LD) and tutoring hours (if purchased).

Parents can also click **Update Profile** to edit their contact details via a popup.

### Registration Forms & Resources

Parents can complete required camp forms such as Family Information, Health Form, Travel Details, and Waivers.
