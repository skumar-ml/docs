### Parent Portal

The portal provides a central place for parents to manage their student’s programs, forms, and resources. It shows all registered students along with their enrolled programs such as camps and tutoring.

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-181.png)
</Frame>

### Student Details

Each student card displays:

- Enrolled programs (e.g., Flagship LD, Texas LD)
- Tutoring hours (if purchased)

Parents can also click **Update Profile** to edit their contact details via a popup.

### Registration Forms & Resources

Parents can complete required camp forms such as Family Information, Health Form, Travel Details, and Waivers.

<Frame>
  ![Image](/images/image-182.png)
</Frame>

<Frame>
  
</Frame>

A progress tracker shows how many forms are completed.

- Clicking **Go to Form** opens the form for completion.
- Clicking **Go to Invoice** opens invoice details in a popup.
